AtSea Qibla — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

AtSea Qibla ("the app") is a Qibla direction, prayer-time and prayer-alert companion for seafarers, published by Hisyam of HMK Solutions ("we"). The app has no user accounts, advertising or analytics. Most features work fully offline, and what you record in the app stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

Information kept on your phone

The app saves your settings, positions, ship heading entries, prayer records, journal notes, voyages, photos you add and alert settings in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive this information. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location (GPS) while the app is in use, to calculate the Qibla bearing, prayer times and the ship's course. You can enter a position by hand or read it from a photo instead. The app does not use location in the background.

Photos and camera

If you read a position or heading from a photo (for example of a bridge GPS or gyro display), the text is recognised on your device with Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The photo is not uploaded.

Online services the app contacts

When you are online, some features contact these services. Each receives your device's IP address and the request below, under its own privacy policy.

  • BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.com) — your coordinates, to identify the country and suggest a prayer-time calculation method.
  • OpenStreetMap (openstreetmap.org) — the map area you view, to download map tiles.
  • NOAA Tides and Currents (tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov) — only the tide station number you enter, when you ask for tide predictions.
  • GitHub (github.com) — to download Azan audio recordings you choose from the catalogue.
  • Google Firebase (firebase.google.com) — only if you rate an Azan recording. The app signs in anonymously (no name, email or location) and stores your star rating with a random app ID so each phone has one vote per recording. The combined ratings are downloaded to show a ranking.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts are scheduled on your phone. They do not send any information off the device.

Links and donations

Links to hadith and Quran websites, WhatsApp, and donation details for a surau campaign open outside the app. The app does not process payments; any donation is made in your own banking app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete everything kept on your phone. To delete your anonymous Azan ratings, contact us at the address below with the approximate date you rated.
